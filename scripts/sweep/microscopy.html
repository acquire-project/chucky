Reading the study

Throughput counts logical image bytes per second through final close. Compression fold divides logical input bytes by physical output bytes. Spatial padding is retained in configuration details, so it cannot inflate either objective.

A configuration is on the observed frontier when no other tested setting in the same study, image input, backend, and sink is at least as good at both objectives and better at one. Filtering recomputes that frontier among the remaining settings. Raw controls participate in the same comparison.

Discovery measurements identify candidates for confirmation. One observation does not establish repeatability; min–max ranges and the reference-drift threshold are observations, not confidence intervals. Pilot results are labelled separately and estimate execution cost.

Blosc blocks subdivide compression within each chunk. Requested block sizes are recorded explicitly; the compressor may adjust its actual layout. CPU raw LZ4 uses an HC entry point, whereas CPU Blosc-LZ4 uses LZ4, so their difference includes more than framing.

Pipeline stage intervals overlap. Their times and rates help explain limits but do not sum to elapsed time. Study measurements are separate from the regular hero and regression sweeps in Over time.
